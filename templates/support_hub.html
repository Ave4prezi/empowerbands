How the groups will work

  • Members join by invitation; group discussions are visible only to approved members and moderators.
  • April and Avery moderate initially and can appoint additional moderators in admin.
  • Members can report a message or leave a group.
  • Members should avoid posting phone numbers, addresses, medical records, or someone else’s personal details.
  • The groups offer peer support, not counseling or emergency response.
