EmpowerBands Pet Finder · For dogs and cats

A little tag.
A way back home.

Pet Finder is an NFC identification tag. Tap it with an NFC-compatible phone to open your pet’s profile and the contact information you approved for sharing.

Pet Finder is not a GPS tracker.

Three steps to connect

1. Activate

Use the serial number and activation code included in your package.

2. Add your pet

Create a pet profile and choose which information to share.

3. Tap to connect

A finder taps the tag with a compatible phone to see the information you approved.

No battery. No charging. No special app required.
