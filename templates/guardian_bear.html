Good to know

Before your first tap.

Which phones can read the Safety Bear?

A compatible NFC-enabled smartphone can read the embedded module. Reader position, settings, and phone cases vary. The phone needs internet access to load the web profile; no special EmpowerBands app is required.

Where do I tap my phone?

Follow the instructions included with your bear, or ask our team to show you the module’s location. Hold the phone’s NFC reader close to that spot and open the notification.

Who can see the Safety Profile?

Anyone opening the linked profile can see the public information you enter. Private medical notes require the profile PIN. Review the public view before sharing your bear and keep activation codes private.

Does the Safety Bear have GPS, a camera, or a microphone?

No. The NFC module opens a web profile when tapped. It does not provide live GPS tracking, record conversations, or take pictures. Any supported phone-location sharing is a separate action that requires permission on the phone.

Can I update contacts or instructions later?

Yes. Open Manage My Profile and complete the verification steps. Keep caregiver contacts and care instructions current.

How should I clean the bear?

Follow the bear’s care label and confirm cleaning guidance with EmpowerBands before washing a bear with an embedded module. A waterproof or machine-washable rating has not been published. Test the NFC tap after cleaning.

Does it contact emergency services automatically?

No. Safety Bear connects to a Safety Profile using a compatible phone and internet access. It does not replace adult supervision or contact emergency services automatically. In an emergency, call 911 directly.
