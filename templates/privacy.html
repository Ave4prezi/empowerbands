Privacy Policy

EmpowerBands collects limited profile information to support emergency response, caregiver communication, and safety alerts.

Information may include a name, emergency contacts, medical notes, instructions, and optional profile details entered by a user, guardian, school, or caregiver.

SMS alerts are used only for emergency-related notifications connected to an activated EmpowerBand profile. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

EmpowerBands does not sell personal information.
